The three motion rules

All three app animations below obey arstro.rule.animation.

1. Never stretchParts only move and fade. No bar grows, no sun is squashed. Streaks keep their length.
2. Speed has a slopeArriving parts slow into rest. Leaving parts start slowly from rest and pull away.
3. Never vanish suddenlyEvery leaving part fades while it is still moving, and reaches zero as its move ends.
