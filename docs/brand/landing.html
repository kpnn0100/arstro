Arstro is art plus astro: reach the star by precise engineering. It is an indie project of three creative apps, one for photo, one for video and one for sound.

None of them stands on a borrowed core. The drawing, the image maths and the sound all come from engines written here, so every app looks, moves and answers in the same way.

This page is a flight past all three. Scroll, and the ship does the rest: each planet stops you, shows what its app does, and lets you go.

NO. 2PHOTOIN DEVELOPMENT
cosmo

The professional photo editor that reads a photo before it touches it.

Cosmo starts where a careful photographer starts: by reading the photo. It measures how the picture was shot, its light, its range and its colour cast, before it changes a pixel. Then it corrects what the camera got wrong, and only after that does it give the photo a look.

The planet on this page is that photo. Scroll and watch it be read, corrected and graded, then drag across it to compare. Cosmo runs on its own image engine, on Linux today, with an Android build on the way.

  • ONE PHOTOA RAW and the JPEG beside it open as one photo.
  • YOUR WORDSAsk for a look in your own words and get the edit.
  • ONE STEPEvery edit is one step you can undo.

NO. 3VIDEOIN DEVELOPMENT
interstellar

The video colour tool that can cut.

Interstellar is a colour tool first and an editor second. It groups the shots that belong to one scene, grades them together, cuts what does not belong and delivers the film. The colour comes from a live Cosmo project, so a still and a moving picture are graded by the same hand.

A timeline here is a version. You can override one, pin it, freeze it or rebase it on another. Every parameter has an address, and anything with an address can be automated. The planet beside this column wears its timeline as bands of cloud.

  • ONE AUTHORITYHosts a live Cosmo project for its colour.
  • VERSIONSOverride, pin, freeze and rebase a timeline.
  • ADDRESSESEvery parameter can be automated.
NO. 4SOUNDIN DEVELOPMENT
solaris

The digital audio workstation at the centre of the system.

Solaris is the star the other two turn around: a studio where a song is arranged, mixed and played. Every sample and every instrument gets its own mixer strip, so nothing hides inside a group and every sound can be reached.

Its instruments and effects are not bought in. They are built in Arstro's own sound engine, down to a piano made from the physics of its strings. Automation and formulas work on any parameter. Under the star, the dots are first notes, then meters.

  • A STRIP EACHEvery sample and instrument has its own strip.
  • OWN ENGINEInstruments and effects are built here.
  • FORMULASAutomation and formulas on any parameter.
NO. 5THE ENGINE ROOMBACK PAGE

The apps are the ships.
These are the engines.

Three libraries carry everything above. Each one works on its own, with no window and almost no dependencies, which is why three apps can share one look and one way of moving.

DRAWING

Artboard

A 2D drawing and interface framework with no platform code inside. One scene, drawn through a thin adapter to the web, to native, or to a test.

SOUND

Digital Signal Processing

Generators, filters, effects and reverb that plug into each other, and a piano built from the physics of its strings.

IMAGE

Image Processing

Parameters in, pixels out: tone, colour, masks, detail and transforms, on the processor or the graphics card.

Every app can also be driven by text, so a script or an AI agent can do anything the window can.
