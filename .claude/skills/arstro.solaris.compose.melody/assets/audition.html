A melody is eight bars over its chords (one bar of the chords first, the melody, the last chord held); a SONG is a verse, a pre-chorus and a chorus with its words — sung by a voice through a vowel filter, or on a piano. Listen, then judge it — Love it, Good, Okay, Weak or No — and, if you can, say why in a few words ("too busy", "the ending is great", "boring rhythm"). Your verdicts teach the skill.
